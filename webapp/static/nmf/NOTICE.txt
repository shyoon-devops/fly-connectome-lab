Local modifications: MaleCNS whole-connectome WebSocket bridge, autonomous
brain control mode, neural telemetry HUD, target waypoints, and parent dashboard
integration. The interactive flight mode applies a transparent quasi-steady
lift/thrust/yaw force model to the MuJoCo free body and animates the supplied
wing geometry; it is not a CFD or measured wing-aerodynamics solver. Placeable
stimulus objects are experimental input generators, not source-dataset objects.
